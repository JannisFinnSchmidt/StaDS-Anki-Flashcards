What is an isocline (Isokline) of a first-order ODE?	The set of points where the slope field has one fixed value c. It is found by setting the right-hand side equal to c and solving for the curve.	ODE geometry direction-field
How do isoclines help you understand an ODE qualitatively?	They let you sketch the direction field without solving anything: along each isocline all solution curves cross with the same slope, revealing the shape of the solution family.	ODE geometry direction-field
What is the Euler method for an ODE?	Step forward along the tangent: the new value is the old value plus the step size times the right-hand side evaluated at the current point. It produces the Euler polygon.<br><br>\[ y_{k+1}=y_k+h\,f(t_k,y_k) \]	ODE numerics euler
What is the separation of variables method?	For an equation whose right-hand side factors into a function of x times a function of y, divide by the y-part and integrate both sides separately.<br><br>\[ y'=g(x)h(y)\ \Rightarrow\ \int\frac{dy}{h(y)}=\int g(x)\,dx \]	ODE solution-methods separation
What is variation of constants (Variation der Konstanten)?	Solve the homogeneous equation first, then allow its integration constant to become a function of x, insert into the full equation and solve for that function.	ODE solution-methods variation-of-constants
What is the general structure of the solution set of a linear inhomogeneous ODE?	One particular solution plus the full set of homogeneous solutions. The solution set is an affine space, not a vector space.<br><br>\[ y=y_{p}+y_{h},\qquad y_h\in\ker(L) \]	ODE structure linearity
Why do homogeneous linear ODE solutions form a vector space but inhomogeneous ones do not?	Because a sum of homogeneous solutions is again a solution, whereas the sum of two inhomogeneous solutions produces twice the right-hand side. The inhomogeneous set does not contain zero.	ODE structure linearity
What is the dimension of the solution space of a homogeneous linear first-order ODE?	One. A single integration constant parameterizes all solutions.	ODE structure dimension
What is the dimension of the solution space of a homogeneous linear second-order ODE?	Two. Two independent constants are fixed by the initial value and the initial derivative.	ODE structure dimension
What is the dimension of the solution space of a homogeneous linear first-order system of n equations?	n. Each component of the initial vector contributes one degree of freedom.	ODE systems dimension
What is a Bernoulli differential equation?	An equation with an extra power of y beyond the linear terms, with exponent not 0 or 1. It is non-linear but reducible to a linear equation.<br><br>\[ y'+p(x)y=q(x)\,y^{\alpha},\qquad \alpha\neq 0,1 \]	ODE solution-methods bernoulli
How is a Bernoulli equation solved?	Substitute u equal to y raised to the power 1 minus alpha. This transforms it into a linear first-order equation in u, which is then solved and transformed back.<br><br>\[ u=y^{1-\alpha}\ \Rightarrow\ u'+(1-\alpha)p(x)u=(1-\alpha)q(x) \]	ODE solution-methods bernoulli
How is an equation whose right-hand side depends on a linear combination ax + by + c solved?	Substitute u equal to that whole linear combination. The result is a separable equation in u.	ODE solution-methods substitution
How is a homogeneous-type equation depending only on the ratio y over x solved?	Substitute u equal to y divided by x. The resulting equation in u is separable.	ODE solution-methods substitution
What is an exact differential equation?	One that can be written as the total derivative of a potential function F, so that solutions are exactly the level curves of F.<br><br>\[ P(x,y)\,dx+Q(x,y)\,dy=0,\qquad dF=P\,dx+Q\,dy \]	ODE exact-equations
What is the necessary and sufficient criterion (Satz von Schwarz) for exactness?	The partial derivative of the first coefficient with respect to y must equal the partial derivative of the second with respect to x. Mixed second derivatives of a potential must agree.<br><br>\[ \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} \]	ODE exact-equations
Why does every solution of an exact equation lie on a level curve of F?	Because the chain rule shows the total derivative of F along a solution is exactly the left-hand side of the equation, hence zero. F is therefore constant along solutions.	ODE exact-equations
How do you construct the potential function F for an exact equation?	Integrate the first coefficient with respect to x, letting the integration constant depend on y, then differentiate the result with respect to y and match it against the second coefficient.	ODE exact-equations
What is a global Lipschitz condition?	There is one constant L that bounds the change in the right-hand side relative to the change in y, uniformly over the whole domain.<br><br>\[ \|f(t,y_1)-f(t,y_2)\|\ \le\ L\,\|y_1-y_2\| \]	ODE existence lipschitz
What is a local Lipschitz condition?	The Lipschitz bound only has to hold on some neighbourhood of each point, with a constant that may vary from neighbourhood to neighbourhood.	ODE existence lipschitz
What is the practical way to verify a Lipschitz condition?	Check that the partial derivative with respect to y is bounded. A bounded y-derivative implies the Lipschitz condition via the mean value theorem.	ODE existence lipschitz
What is a classic example of a function that is not Lipschitz, and what goes wrong?	The square root of y at zero: its derivative blows up there, and the corresponding initial value problem has more than one solution.<br><br>\[ y'=\sqrt{y},\ y(0)=0:\quad y\equiv 0\ \text{ and }\ y=\tfrac{t^2}{4} \]	ODE existence uniqueness
What is a Banach space?	A complete normed vector space: every Cauchy sequence in it converges to a limit that is still in the space.	ODE functional-analysis banach
Why is the space of continuous functions on a compact interval with the supremum norm a Banach space?	Because uniform convergence preserves continuity, so the limit of a uniformly Cauchy sequence of continuous functions is itself continuous and lies in the space.	ODE functional-analysis banach
What is the difference between pointwise and uniform convergence?	Pointwise convergence allows the required index to depend on the point; uniform convergence requires one index that works for every point simultaneously.	ODE analysis convergence
Why does uniform convergence preserve continuity but pointwise convergence does not?	Because the uniform bound lets you split the estimate into three small pieces independently of the point, which is exactly the three-epsilon argument.	ODE analysis convergence
State the Banach fixed point theorem.	A contraction of a Banach space into itself has exactly one fixed point, and the fixed point iteration converges to it from any starting point.	ODE fixed-point banach
What does the Picard-Lindelof theorem state?	If the right-hand side is continuous and satisfies a Lipschitz condition in y, the initial value problem has exactly one solution on an interval around the initial point.<br><br>\[ f\ \text{continuous}+\text{Lipschitz in }y\ \Rightarrow\ \exists!\,y\ \text{on }[t_0-\delta,t_0+\delta] \]	ODE existence picard-lindelof
What is the integral operator used in the proof of Picard-Lindelof?	The map sending a function to the initial value plus the integral of the right-hand side evaluated along that function. Its fixed points are exactly the solutions of the initial value problem.<br><br>\[ (\Phi y)(t)=y_0+\int_{t_0}^{t}f\big(s,y(s)\big)\,ds \]	ODE existence picard-lindelof
Why is the initial value problem rewritten as an integral equation?	Because the integral form turns the differential equation into a fixed point problem, to which the Banach fixed point theorem can be applied directly.	ODE existence picard-lindelof
What is Picard iteration?	Repeatedly applying the integral operator starting from a constant function. Each iteration produces a better approximation converging to the true solution.<br><br>\[ y_{n+1}(t)=y_0+\int_{t_0}^{t}f\big(s,y_n(s)\big)\,ds \]	ODE existence picard-iteration
What is the difference between local and global Picard-Lindelof?	The local version guarantees a solution only on some possibly tiny neighbourhood; the global version, with a global Lipschitz condition, guarantees it on the whole prescribed interval.	ODE existence picard-lindelof
What is the maximal interval of existence?	The largest connected interval containing the initial point on which the solution exists. A solution may blow up in finite time and stop existing beyond that.	ODE existence
What is a fundamental system for a second-order linear ODE?	A pair of linearly independent solutions of the homogeneous equation. Every solution is a linear combination of them.	ODE linear-systems fundamental-system
What is the initial value vector (Anfangswertvektor) of a solution?	The vector containing the function value and the derivative value at the initial point. It fully determines the solution.	ODE linear-systems
How is linear independence of two solutions checked via initial value vectors?	Two solutions are linearly independent exactly when their initial value vectors are linearly independent. Independence at one point transfers to the whole interval.	ODE linear-systems fundamental-system
Why does linear independence of the initial value vectors imply independence of the solutions?	Because uniqueness of the initial value problem means that a vanishing linear combination must have vanishing initial data, which forces the coefficients to be zero.	ODE linear-systems fundamental-system
Does linear independence of two arbitrary functions imply independence of their initial value vectors?	No. Two functions can be independent as functions while sharing proportional data at one point; the equivalence only holds for solutions of the same linear ODE.	ODE linear-systems
How is a homogeneous linear second-order ODE with constant coefficients solved?	Write down the characteristic quadratic equation and solve for its roots. The form of the general solution depends on whether the roots are real distinct, real repeated, or complex.<br><br>\[ y''+ay'+by=0\ \Rightarrow\ \lambda^2+a\lambda+b=0 \]	ODE constant-coefficients characteristic-equation
What is the solution form for two distinct real characteristic roots?	A linear combination of two exponentials, one for each root.<br><br>\[ y=c_1e^{\lambda_1 t}+c_2e^{\lambda_2 t} \]	ODE constant-coefficients
What is the solution form for a repeated real characteristic root?	One exponential plus x times the same exponential. The extra factor of x supplies the missing second independent solution.<br><br>\[ y=c_1e^{\lambda t}+c_2\,t\,e^{\lambda t} \]	ODE constant-coefficients
What is the solution form for complex conjugate characteristic roots?	An exponential of the real part multiplied by a cosine and a sine of the imaginary part. Oscillation with an exponential envelope.<br><br>\[ y=e^{\alpha t}\big(c_1\cos\beta t+c_2\sin\beta t\big),\qquad \lambda=\alpha\pm i\beta \]	ODE constant-coefficients
How are real solutions extracted from complex ones?	Take the real and imaginary parts of the complex solution. Both are real solutions because the equation has real coefficients and is linear.	ODE constant-coefficients complex
How is an inhomogeneous linear ODE with constant coefficients solved?	Solve the homogeneous equation, guess a particular solution of the same type as the right-hand side, insert and compare coefficients, then add the two and fit the initial conditions.	ODE constant-coefficients ansatz
What ansatz is used when the right-hand side is a polynomial?	A general polynomial of the same degree with unknown coefficients, determined by inserting and comparing coefficients.	ODE constant-coefficients ansatz
What ansatz is used when the right-hand side is a cosine or sine?	A linear combination of both a cosine and a sine with the same frequency, since differentiation mixes the two.	ODE constant-coefficients ansatz
What must be done if the ansatz already solves the homogeneous equation?	Multiply the ansatz by x (or a higher power) so it is no longer a homogeneous solution. Otherwise inserting it yields zero and the coefficients cannot be determined.	ODE constant-coefficients ansatz resonance
How is a higher-order ODE converted into a first-order system?	Introduce new variables for each successive derivative. An n-th order equation becomes a system of n first-order equations.<br><br>\[ z_1=y,\ z_2=y',\dots,z_n=y^{(n-1)}\ \Rightarrow\ z'=Az \]	ODE systems reduction
Why is the reduction to a first-order system useful?	Because existence and uniqueness theory, as well as numerical methods, are formulated for first-order systems and then apply to any order.	ODE systems reduction
How is a homogeneous linear system with constant coefficients solved?	Make the exponential ansatz with an unknown vector; inserting it shows the vector must be an eigenvector and the exponent the corresponding eigenvalue.<br><br>\[ y'=Ay,\quad y=e^{\lambda t}v\ \Rightarrow\ Av=\lambda v \]	ODE systems eigenvalues
What is the general solution of a linear system when the matrix has a full eigenvector basis?	A linear combination of exponentials of each eigenvalue times its eigenvector, with one free constant per eigenvector.<br><br>\[ y(t)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}c_i\,e^{\lambda_i t}\,v_i \]	ODE systems eigenvalues
How are real solutions built from complex eigenvalues of a real system?	Take the real and imaginary parts of the complex solution, which combine the exponential of the real part with cosines and sines and the real and imaginary parts of the eigenvector.<br><br>\[ y_1=\operatorname{Re}\big(e^{\lambda t}v\big),\qquad y_2=\operatorname{Im}\big(e^{\lambda t}v\big) \]	ODE systems complex-eigenvalues
Why do complex eigenvalues of a real matrix come in conjugate pairs?	Because the characteristic polynomial has real coefficients, so its complex roots occur in conjugate pairs, and the eigenvectors are conjugate as well.	ODE systems complex-eigenvalues
What is the matrix exponential and how is it defined?	The power series of matrix powers over factorials, exactly mirroring the scalar exponential. It converges for every square matrix.<br><br>\[ e^{At}=\sum_{k\ge 0}\frac{(At)^k}{k!} \]	ODE matrix-exponential
Why do the columns of the matrix exponential form a fundamental system?	Because its derivative with respect to x equals the matrix times itself, so each column solves the system, and invertibility of the exponential makes the columns independent.<br><br>\[ \frac{d}{dt}e^{At}=A\,e^{At} \]	ODE matrix-exponential
When does the product rule for matrix exponentials hold?	Only when the two matrices commute. Without commutativity the binomial expansion of the series fails.<br><br>\[ e^{A+B}=e^Ae^B\quad\text{iff}\quad AB=BA \]	ODE matrix-exponential
What is the inverse of a matrix exponential?	The exponential of the negated matrix. The matrix exponential is therefore always invertible.<br><br>\[ \big(e^{A}\big)^{-1}=e^{-A} \]	ODE matrix-exponential
How is the matrix exponential computed when the matrix is diagonalizable?	Diagonalize, exponentiate the eigenvalues on the diagonal, and transform back. The series collapses to scalar exponentials.<br><br>\[ A=S\Lambda S^{-1}\ \Rightarrow\ e^{At}=S\,e^{\Lambda t}\,S^{-1} \]	ODE matrix-exponential
How is the matrix exponential computed for a Jordan block?	Split the matrix into a diagonal part plus a nilpotent part; the two commute, so the exponentials multiply, and the nilpotent series terminates after finitely many terms.	ODE matrix-exponential nilpotent
What is the Lotka-Volterra predator-prey model?	A pair of coupled non-linear ODEs where the prey grows exponentially but is reduced by encounters, and the predator decays but is fed by those same encounters.<br><br>\[ \dot x=\alpha x-\beta xy,\qquad \dot y=-\gamma y+\delta xy \]	ODE modelling lotka-volterra
What does the product term in the Lotka-Volterra model represent?	The rate of encounters between predators and prey, which is proportional to the product of the two population sizes.	ODE modelling lotka-volterra
What is a stationary point of a dynamical system?	A state where all derivatives vanish, so the system stays there forever. For Lotka-Volterra it is the ratio of the two parameter pairs.<br><br>\[ \big(x^*,y^*\big)=\Big(\tfrac{\gamma}{\delta},\ \tfrac{\alpha}{\beta}\Big) \]	ODE dynamics equilibrium
What is the conserved quantity of the Lotka-Volterra system?	A specific combination of the two populations and their logarithms which stays constant along every solution. It is the first integral of the system.<br><br>\[ V(x,y)=\delta x-\gamma\log x+\beta y-\alpha\log y=\text{const} \]	ODE dynamics conservation
What does the existence of a conserved quantity imply for Lotka-Volterra solutions?	All solutions are periodic: they move along closed level curves of the conserved quantity rather than converging or diverging.	ODE dynamics conservation
Why is the stationary point a global minimum of the Lotka-Volterra conserved quantity?	Because its gradient vanishes exactly there and its Hessian is positive definite everywhere, so the function is convex with a single minimum.	ODE dynamics conservation
What is a resonance curve in a driven oscillator?	The amplitude of the steady-state response plotted as a function of the driving frequency. It peaks where the driving frequency approaches the natural frequency.	ODE oscillations resonance
How is the steady-state amplitude of a driven oscillator computed?	Use a complex exponential ansatz, insert into the equation, and compare real and imaginary parts to extract the amplitude and the phase shift.	ODE oscillations resonance
What is the phase shift in a driven oscillator?	The lag between the driving force and the response, determined by the ratio of the damping term to the difference between the natural and driving frequencies.	ODE oscillations resonance
What is the Lipschitz condition for a linear system, and what does it require?	The bound is given by the matrix norm of the coefficient matrix. It is satisfied whenever that matrix is continuous on a compact interval.	ODE systems lipschitz
Why does every linear ODE system with continuous coefficients have global solutions?	Because the Lipschitz condition holds globally with the maximum matrix norm as constant, so Picard-Lindelof applies on the whole interval with no blow-up.	ODE systems existence
How is the Lipschitz condition checked for a non-linear system?	Bound the norm of the Jacobian of the right-hand side with respect to the state vector. A bounded Jacobian gives the Lipschitz constant.	ODE systems lipschitz jacobian
What does a zero determinant of the coefficient matrix imply for a linear system?	Zero is an eigenvalue, so there is a non-trivial constant solution along the corresponding eigenvector. Constant solutions exist exactly when the matrix is singular.	ODE systems eigenvalues
What is the long-run behaviour when the coefficient matrix has eigenvalues of both signs?	The solution generally blows up: the component along the positive eigenvalue grows without bound while the negative one decays, so the norm diverges.	ODE systems stability
Does a zero trace imply that all eigenvalues are real?	No. A zero trace only means the eigenvalues sum to zero, which is also satisfied by a purely imaginary conjugate pair.	ODE linear-algebra eigenvalues
How do you show that exponential-times-eigenvector solutions are linearly independent?	Set a general linear combination to zero and evaluate at time zero; independence of the eigenvectors then forces all coefficients to vanish.	ODE systems fundamental-system
What is the vector space criterion used throughout for solution sets?	The set must be non-empty, closed under addition, and closed under scalar multiplication. Checking these three points is enough to establish it is a subspace.	ODE structure linearity
What is the characterization of an orthogonal matrix?	Its transpose equals its inverse, equivalently its columns form an orthonormal basis. It preserves lengths and angles.<br><br>\[ O^{\top}O=I\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad O^{-1}=O^{\top} \]	ODE linear-algebra orthogonal
Why are eigenvalues of a symmetric real matrix always real?	Working over the complex numbers, the symmetry forces the imaginary parts of the inner products to cancel, which makes the imaginary part of every eigenvalue vanish.	ODE linear-algebra eigenvalues
Why are eigenvectors of distinct eigenvalues of a symmetric matrix orthogonal?	Because symmetry lets the matrix move across the inner product, giving two expressions that differ by the eigenvalue ratio. With distinct eigenvalues, the inner product must be zero.	ODE linear-algebra eigenvalues
What is the principal axis transformation?	The diagonalization of a symmetric matrix by an orthogonal matrix of its eigenvectors, giving the eigenvalues on the diagonal.<br><br>\[ A=S\Lambda S^{\top},\qquad S\ \text{orthogonal} \]	ODE linear-algebra diagonalization
Why is a linear ODE system with a symmetric matrix immediately solvable?	Because symmetry guarantees a full basis of eigenvectors, so the general solution is simply the sum of exponentials times eigenvectors.	ODE systems eigenvalues
What does matrix convergence mean?	Convergence of every entry, equivalently convergence in any matrix norm since all norms on a finite-dimensional space are equivalent.	ODE matrix-analysis convergence
